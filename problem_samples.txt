SEPLAT ENERGY PLC (“SEPLAT ENERGY” OR THE “COMPANY”) 
ANNOUNCEMENT OF CURRENCY EXCHANGE RATE FOR Q2 2026 INTERIM AND SPECIAL 
DIVIDEND 
 
Lagos and London, 13 August 2026 - Seplat Energy Plc ("Seplat Energy" or the "Company") today 
announces the applicable exchange rate for determining the Naira equivalent of the Q2 2026 interim 
dividend and special dividend payable to shareholders who have elected to receive their dividend in 
Naira (₦). 
 
In accordance with the Company's dividend payment procedures, the exchange rate applied is the 
Nigerian Foreign Exchange Market (NFEM) rate published by the Central Bank of Nigeria on 12 
August 2026. 
 
Applicable Exchange Rate: 
US$1 = ₦ 1,360.58 
 
The Naira equivalent of the Q2 2026 interim dividend and special dividend will be calculated using 
the above exchange rate. 
 
Shareholders should refer to the Company’s announcement on July 30, 2026, for the definition of 
default currency on the company website at https://www.seplatenergy.com/investors/regulatory-
announcements/.
